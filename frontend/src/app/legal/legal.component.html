Privacy Policy

Last updated: {{ lastUpdated }}

1. Overview

MotoGuard respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information and vehicle telemetry data in compliance with the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019 and relevant privacy regulations.

2. Information We Collect

We collect account details (name, email address, phone number), vehicle information (license plate, motorcycle model), and device telemetry transmitted by installed hardware (GPS coordinates, speed, heading, ignition status, battery voltage, and 15-digit IMEI).

3. How We Use Your Data

Your information is used solely to provide real-time tracking, generate historical ride breadcrumbs, calculate geofence breach notifications, detect vehicle tampering, and maintain service security. We do not sell or rent your personal or location data to third parties.

4. Data Sharing

We only share telemetry with essential service providers: cellular carriers for SIM connectivity and secure cloud hosting infrastructure. In cases of motorcycle theft or legal investigation, location vectors may be shared with law enforcement authorities upon verified request.

5. Data Retention

High-resolution location breadcrumbs are retained for 90 days for operational replay. Historical trip aggregates are kept for up to 12 months, after which they are permanently expunged or anonymized.

6. Security Measures

We use industry-standard encryption protocols (TLS 1.3 in transit, salted bcrypt password hashes, and access-controlled databases) to safeguard your vehicle records from unauthorized access.

7. Your Rights

Under the Kenya Data Protection Act, you have the right to access the data we hold about you, request corrections to erroneous records, and request the deletion of your account and associated tracking records upon discontinuing service.

8. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or wish to exercise your data rights, contact us at privacy@motoguard.co.ke.
