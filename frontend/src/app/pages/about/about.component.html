About MGuard

MGuard is a smart motorcycle tracking and anti-theft security system built specifically for Kenyan boda boda riders, delivery couriers, and transport SACCOs. We combine covert security devices, instant mobile alerts, and remote engine shut-off to keep your bike safe and protect your daily livelihood.

Our Story

Boda bodas are a cornerstone of Kenya's economy, yet riders face immense risks of motorcycle theft and loss of daily livelihood.

Traditional trackers are quickly located and cut off by thieves. MGuard offers a completely hidden, tamper-proof alternative that keeps tracking your bike and helps coordinate fast recoveries.

1
The Street Reality


Standard commercial GPS units have conspicuous wires that thieves disconnect in seconds. MGuard uses compact, hidden security devices installed secretly inside the motorcycle frame.

2
Instant Anti-Theft Response


The device stays silent to conserve your battery, and triggers immediate phone alarms the moment any unauthorized movement or tampering occurs, allowing you to turn off the engine remotely.

3
Community & SACCO Support


Accurate route records provide clear proof of theft, coordinating directly with stage leaders and police for swift motorcycle recovery.
