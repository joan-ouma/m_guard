About Us

MGuard is a smart motorcycle tracking and anti-theft security system built specifically for Kenyan boda boda riders. We combine 24/7 intelligent tracking, instant mobile alerts, and remote engine control to keep your bike safe and protect your daily livelihood.

Our Story

Boda bodas are a cornerstone of Kenya's economy, yet riders face immense risks of motorcycle theft and loss of daily livelihood. MGuard provides dependable, always-on protection designed specifically for motorcycles, giving riders full visibility and rapid recovery coordination whenever needed.

  1. The Street Reality

    MGuard is engineered specifically for motorcycles, ensuring discreet integration, extreme durability, and seamless connectivity on the road.

  2. Instant Anti-Theft Response

    Intelligent power management ensures your motorcycle stays protected around the clock, triggering instant alerts upon any unauthorized motion and enabling remote engine control.

  3. Rider Community Support

    Accurate route records provide clear proof of theft, coordinating directly with stage leaders and police for swift motorcycle recovery.
